# Adriatique — 5 Old Sets: SPM (Saves-Per-Million) Analysis

An SPM discovery-intent analysis of tracks Adriatique played across five of their older DJ sets (2013–2017), spanning the Diynamic era into the melodic-techno crossover. SPM = Radio-playlist Saves ÷ Seed-track Streams × 1,000,000.

**Track universe:** ~87 listed entries → **80 unique tracks** after merging cross-set repeats (e.g. *Adriatique – Rollox*, *Midnight Walking*, *Mind Against – Atlant* recurred across sets).

## Scope & method

Same pipeline as the Solomun analysis. Targeted the **discovery-relevant electronic releases** and matched a representative **13-track core** to Spotify with **100% stream coverage** (mystreamcount.com). Excluded unreleased set-only IDs and a Floorplan cut available only as a low-play compilation edit. Of the 13 core tracks, **only 3 have a dedicated Spotify auto-radio** and are scored below; **10 do not.**

## Key findings

- **This is the most underground catalogue of the three DJs analysed.** Only **3 of 13** core tracks (23%) have any dedicated Spotify auto-radio at all — versus 9/15 for the old Solomun sets. Adriatique's 2013–2017 sets are built almost entirely from deep melodic/minimal-house cuts that never entered Spotify's algorithmic-discovery layer.
- **Even the one genuine hit reads as passive.** *Walking With Elephants (Ten Walls)* — the set's breakout crossover at **83.6M streams** — scores just **SPM 10.3** (859 radio saves). Its streams are a mainstream deep-house radio staple; proportionally few are active 'discovery saves' today.
- **Freund Blase (Paul Kalkbrenner) tops the ratio (SPM 29.9)** but on a tiny base — 334K streams, 10 saves. Directional, not scale.
- **Adriatique's own productions and remixes have essentially no radio footprint:** *Modern Talking*, *Something In Between*, *Midnight Walking*, and the *Birth* remix all returned **no dedicated radio**; only their WhoMadeWho *Dreams* remix carries one (SPM 11.9 on 5 saves).
- **Two verification catches (false positives rejected):** a *'Euphrasia' radio* existed but its description named Passenger 10 / SAFARIS / Daniel Portman — a **different** Euphrasia, not Jimi Jules' — so it was marked no-radio; and Jimi Jules' actual release carries only ~15K streams. This mirrors the Eminem *'Shake That'* catch in the Solomun run: title-only radio hits must be artist-confirmed.
- **Structural takeaway holds:** SPM on vintage underground tracks is low because the denominator is lifetime cumulative streams, and for cuts this deep the auto-radio saves — the discovery signal — are often literally single digits or absent.
