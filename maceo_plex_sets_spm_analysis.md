# Maceo Plex — 5 Old Sets: SPM (Saves-Per-Million) Analysis

An SPM discovery-intent analysis of tracks Maceo Plex (Ellum) played across five of his older DJ sets (2011–2015). SPM = Radio-playlist Saves ÷ Seed-track Streams × 1,000,000. Fifth entry in the DJ-set series (Solomun, Adriatique, Tale Of Us, Dixon).

## Scope & method

Same pipeline as the prior four analyses; scored a representative **11-track core** with **100% stream coverage** (mystreamcount.com). Of the 11, **only 4 have a dedicated Spotify auto-radio** and are scored below; **7 do not**.

## Key findings

- **A 'big streams, low discovery-save' profile.** Maceo Plex's own productions are well-streamed — *Conjure Dreams* (17.9M), *Solar Detroit* (5.6M) — but their radio-save rates are modest, so SPM stays low (6–25). His techy, DJ-tool-oriented catalogue gets played more than it gets *collected*.
- **Solar Detroit is the intent leader (SPM 25)** — 142 saves on 5.6M streams — ahead of the *Powers of Ten (Maceo Plex & Shall Ocin Remix)* Bodzin rework (SPM 18).
- **His most famous track is a measurement blind spot.** *Frisky* returned no scoreable radio because the title collides with the **"Frisky Radio" internet station** that saturates search — a data artifact, not a true absence of intent. Flagged, not scored.
- **Radio coverage is sparse (4/11) despite big names.** *Sex Appeal*, *Her Security*, and the deep cuts (*Magma*, *Wasp Factory*, *Blue Earth*, *ISO* — all under 175K streams) have no dedicated auto-radio. Plus many set staples were DJ-only edits/bootlegs never on streaming at all.
- **Cross-artist arc across five DJs (radios per core / SPM range):** euro summer (current pop/dance) 100–400 · **Dixon 9/12, up to SPM 92 (Howling 3,122 saves)** · old Solomun 9/15, 3–100 · old Adriatique 3/13, 10–30 · old Tale Of Us 3/8, 8–60 · **old Maceo Plex 4/11, 6–25**. Maceo Plex lands with the deep/techno cluster on coverage, and lowest on save-rate — heavily streamed, lightly collected.
