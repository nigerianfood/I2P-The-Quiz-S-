## Methodology

**SPM (Saves Per Million)** = Radio Playlist Saves ÷ Seed Track Streams × 1,000,000

For each track: title-only radio search (no artist name), seed verification via the `image_uri` path (`/radio/track/{trackID}/en`), save count from the radio playlist's Spotify page, stream count from the track's Spotify page.

**Methodology notes:**
- This is a rolling "today" chart, so this refresh replaces the 2026-06-26 snapshot. Nine tracks rotated out since June 26 (Rush, Daze, Best Be Believing, Feel, Luv U, and others); nine rotated in (Gotta Be Cool, Shine, Dirty Talk-adjacent entries, Blow Your Mind, Can't Say Nah, etc.).
- Two seeds were recovered from the prior snapshot: Movin' To The Sun (`37i9dQZF1E8NgGaxkzXGLs`) and Chica (`37i9dQZF1E8KDLALFpk2TQ`) — both verified on 2026-06-26 and confirmed still live via playlist description artist match. Radio seeds are stable once created; prior-run verified IDs are a legitimate recovery path when search's 5-result cap buries them.
- 7 tracks have verified radio seeds but no visible stream count anywhere (releases too new for the artist's popular-tracks module). Their saves are recorded; SPM is N/A until streams surface.

**Scale context:** These are current club records mid-promotion. Stream bases move fast — see the Shinjuku/Free Your Mind dilution finding below — so SPM values here are snapshots of a moving target, not settled catalog measurements.

## Key Findings

**How Does It Feel (Dubdogz/FEZZO/Zaark) holds the highest SPM ever measured across all analyses: 536.** 3,193 saves on just 6M streams — one save per 1,866 streams. It was 543 on June 26; a week later the ratio is essentially unchanged, meaning saves are compounding as fast as streams. This is what a record breaking out through active discovery (not playlist placement) looks like.

**The week-over-week comparison is the most valuable data this playlist has produced — SPM dilution identifies which records are being force-fed by playlisting:**
- **Shinjuku: SPM 773 → 118.** Streams grew ~7x (2.7M → 18.3M) in six days while saves grew only 5% (2,050 → 2,151). That stream explosion is editorial/algorithmic placement, not discovery — the new listeners aren't saving the radio.
- **Free Your Mind: SPM 479 → 105.** Same pattern: streams 12.1M → 58.6M (~5x), saves 5,795 → 6,148 (+6%).
- **How Does It Feel: 543 → 536.** Streams and saves growing in lockstep — organic heat.
- **Movin' To The Sun: 235 → 247.** Saves (+27%) outpacing streams (+22%) — still accelerating.
- **I Never Knew: 51 → 70.** Small numbers, but the ratio is improving as the record spreads.

**Jamaican (Bam Bam) set the absolute-saves record: 31,029.** Beats the previous record (Tarzan Boy, 10,830) by nearly 3x. On 211M streams it still ranks 5th at SPM 147 — the rare record with both massive scale and elevated per-stream intent. Note: the June 26 playlist carried a different release ID for this track (no seed found then); today's playlist uses the release that seeds the big radio.

**Talk To You (ANOTR) has 15,018 saves on 116M streams (SPM 130)** — the second-highest absolute save count, and its saves grew ~400/week while holding ratio. A settled club anthem still pulling discovery.

**Suavecito (Malo, 1972) at SPM 81 is the anomaly** — a 53-year-old Latin rock ballad on a Beatport chart snapshot (likely from an edit/sample in circulation), with genuinely healthy radio-save intent on its 38.5M-stream catalog.

**Seven tracks are too new to score:** Dreams (Prospa, 164 saves — up from 109 on June 26, clearly building), Not Exactly - Rinzen Remix (50 saves), Shine (HUGEL/Guetta, 36 saves), Gotta Be Cool (25), Warpdrive (15), Can't Say Nah (8), Blow Your Mind (3). All have verified seeds; stream counts haven't surfaced on their artist pages yet. Dreams and Shine are the ones to re-check first.
