**Playlist:** [euro summer ⛱️](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/4ckFevVILxjzS5Hi11Lo1K) · Playlist saves: 19

**Analyzed:** 2026-07-15 · **Tracks resolved:** 46 of 50 (4 tracks were truncated by the page renderer and could not be captured)

**Stream data coverage:** 46/46 (100%) via mystreamcount.com · **Radios found & scored:** 39 (28 direct-seed + 11 duplicate-release seed, all description-confirmed) · **No dedicated radio:** 7


**SPM = Radio-playlist Saves ÷ Seed-track Streams × 1,000,000.** Higher SPM = listeners who hear the track actively save/collect it (discovery/intent signal) rather than passively streaming it.

## Key Findings

- **Benediction (Hot Natured) — SPM 386** is the runaway intent leader: only ~7.9M streams but 3,065 radio saves. A cult favourite that heavily over-indexes on collection relative to plays.
- **The Rapture Pt.III (&ME, Black Coffee, Keinemusik) — SPM 136** is the best "big + sticky" signal on the list: 131M streams *and* 17,883 radio saves. High reach with high intent is rare.
- **Yamore (MoBlack, Salif Keita…) — SPM 111** posts the highest raw save count of any scored track (31,537 saves), pairing scale with strong intent — a genuine Afro-house anchor.
- **Ma Tnsani (Yalla Habibi) — SPM 118** and **Les Gout (Rampa, Keinemusik) — SPM 90** confirm the Afro/melodic-house core of this playlist reads as active-discovery, not background.
- **Low-intent-despite-scale:** *Unforgettable* (French Montana/Swae Lee, SPM 10.5 on ~3.0B streams), *Karibu* (SPM 6), and *Ke Shy* (SPM 6.9) are passive catalog plays — streamed, rarely collected.
- **Electronic dominance holds:** every track scoring SPM ≥ 60 is a house/Afro-house/melodic-electronic cut, reinforcing that the discovery signal on this playlist lives in dance music, not the pop/rap catalog entries.
- **Cross-reference:** *Talk To You (ANOTR)* (#41) and *Jamaican – Bam Bam (HUGEL)* (#44) also appeared in the earlier Beatport analysis; both read as moderate-intent here (SPM 13.7 and 9.5).
