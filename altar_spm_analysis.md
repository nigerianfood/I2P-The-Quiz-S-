## Methodology

**SPM (Saves Per Million)** = Radio Playlist Saves ÷ Seed Track Streams × 1,000,000

Sequenced streams-first (per request) on this 100-track set: every play count was resolved first (100/100) via the mystreamcount.com API, then title-only radio discovery, seed verification (`image_uri` path `/radio/track/{trackID}/en`), and save-count fetches were layered on. Full procedure in `SPM_METHODOLOGY.md`.

**Coverage note.** Altar is a deep alternative-electronic playlist: ~70 of 100 tracks sit under 300K lifetime streams and many are one-word/common titles. **44 tracks have a dedicated auto-generated radio** (verified seed) and are scored below. **56 tracks surfaced no dedicated radio in title-only search** — either the title returns no auto-radio at all (Spotify doesn't mint one for many ultra-underground cuts) or the title is common enough that another artist's song owns every result. Those 56 are listed at the end with their (fully resolved) stream counts; they are queued for the deeper version-qualified / description-sweep / Google pass but are reported honestly as unscored here rather than guessed.

**Two duplicate-release seeds** were accepted per methodology 4b — the radio seeds a different release ID of the *same* song and the radio description names the track's artist: **DIGITiSE** (desc "With HAAi…") and **Plastic Camelia** (desc "With Olof Dreijer…").

**Scale caveat.** Many scored tracks have micro stream bases (sub-100K). A handful of saves over a tiny denominator yields a large SPM that is directional, not scale-comparable to the head. Read within the underground context and lean on absolute saves for cross-track weight.

## Key Findings

**Return To Bhibo (Barry Can't Swim) is the strongest data point at real scale (SPM 114).** 336 radio saves on 2.9M streams — high on both axes, the cleanest "actually breaking through discovery" signal in the set.

**Me and You (Bonobo) leads the mid-scale tier at SPM 121** — 168 saves on 1.4M streams. A known-name track still pulling genuine radio-save intent rather than passive plays.

**Comes and Goes (KETTAMA) owns the absolute-saves crown (928) but only SPM 92** — its 10.1M streams (the playlist's largest denominator) compress the ratio. Real record, diluted quotient.

**The top of the SPM table is micro-base noise.** MY SEARCH IS OVER (353), Situationz (113) and Clip Empty (104) each ride 1–3 saves over sub-10K stream bases. The ratios are arithmetically real but fragile — exactly the small-numerator/small-denominator effect flagged in the methodology.

**Eight scored tracks have zero-save radios** (Maayam, Motorbike Rider, I Think I'm About To Break, 138 Perc, hellooooo????, Gimme A Break A Second, DUMBB, Driving On Sand). The auto-radios exist and seed correctly, but no listener has engaged yet — normal for very fresh underground releases.

**The back half of the playlist is a radio desert.** Of tracks 40–100, the large majority surface no dedicated auto-radio at all — Spotify doesn't generate one for the deepest cuts, and common one-word titles (Crazy, Lonely, Danger, Better, Rise Up, So High) are owned by other artists in search. This is the structural blind spot for a curator-driven alt-electronic list, not a data gap in the streams (all 100 stream counts are resolved).
