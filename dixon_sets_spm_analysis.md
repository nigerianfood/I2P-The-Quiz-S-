# Dixon — 5 Old Sets: SPM (Saves-Per-Million) Analysis

An SPM discovery-intent analysis of tracks Dixon (Innervisions) played across five of his older DJ sets (2011–2015). SPM = Radio-playlist Saves ÷ Seed-track Streams × 1,000,000. Fourth entry in the DJ-set series alongside Solomun, Adriatique, and Tale Of Us.

## Scope & method

Same pipeline as the prior three analyses; scored a representative **12-track core** with **100% stream coverage** (mystreamcount.com). Of the 12, **9 have a dedicated Spotify auto-radio** and are scored below; **3 do not**.

## Key findings

- **Dixon's catalogue is by far the most discoverable of the series.** 9 of 12 core tracks carry a dedicated Spotify auto-radio — versus 3/13 for Adriatique and 3/8 for Tale Of Us. His Innervisions-centric deep/melodic house became genuine streaming catalogue, not just club-only underground.
- **Howling (Âme Remix) — Frank Wiedemann & RY X is the anchor of the entire series.** At **3,122 radio saves** it has the most absolute saves of any track scored across all four old-set analyses, and at SPM 73 on 42.7M streams it pairs mass reach with genuine ongoing intent — one of the defining melodic-house records of the 2010s.
- **The Âme-remix cluster over-indexes on intent:** *This Is Not The End* (SPM 92 — highest ratio, micro-base 120K streams), *Envision* (63), plus Howling. Âme's remix touch reliably produces high save-rates.
- **David August's Epikur is a scale standout:** 992 saves on 20.6M streams (SPM 48) — a deep cut that genuinely crossed over.
- **Only the common-title cuts fall through:** *The Edge* (Frankey & Sandrino), *Moving* (Petar Dundov) and the *Leave House* MCDE-remix release have no dedicated radio — the exceptions, not the rule here.
- **Cross-artist arc across four analyses:** euro summer (current pop/dance) SPM 100–400 → **Dixon: 9/12 core have radios, SPM 5–92, anchored by the 3,122-save Howling** → old Solomun 3–100 (9/15) → old Adriatique 10–30 (3/13) → old Tale Of Us 8–60 (3/8). Dixon is the most 'streaming-legible' of the underground selectors — his picks aged into catalogue.
