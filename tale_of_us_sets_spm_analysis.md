# Tale Of Us — 5 Old Sets: SPM (Saves-Per-Million) Analysis

An SPM discovery-intent analysis of tracks Tale Of Us played across five of their older DJ sets (2013–2016), the Life and Death → Afterlife melodic-techno era. SPM = Radio-playlist Saves ÷ Seed-track Streams × 1,000,000. Part of the same series as the Solomun and Adriatique analyses.

**Track universe:** ~89 listed entries → **66 unique tracks** after merging cross-set repeats (e.g. *Mano Le Tough – Primitive People (Tale Of Us Remix)* appeared in both 2013 sets; Ten Walls and Recondite recur throughout).

## Scope & method

Same pipeline as the prior two analyses; scored a representative electronic core with **100% stream coverage** (mystreamcount.com). **This catalogue was by far the hardest to match** — a large share of the tracks are not on streaming at all (Recondite's catalogue is licensor-restricted on Spotify; *Tale Of Us – Another Earth / Obscure Promises*, *Mind Against – Elysium / Eclipse*, and *Vermont – Übersprung* returned nothing), which is itself the headline finding. Of the **8 core tracks** that matched, **only 3 have a dedicated Spotify auto-radio**; **5 do not**.

## Key findings

- **The deepest, most stream-invisible catalogue of the series.** Beyond the 5 core tracks with no radio, a big fraction of what Tale Of Us actually played isn't on Spotify in any form — Recondite (restricted) plus multiple Life and Death / Minus cuts. For a discovery metric built on Spotify's auto-radios, much of this music simply has no footprint to measure.
- **The melodic/crossover cuts are where intent concentrates.** *Dead End Thrills — Cubicolor (Patrice Bäumel Remix)* tops the table at **SPM 60** (133 saves on 2.2M streams), and *Singularity — Stephan Bodzin* pairs the most absolute saves (**1,174**) with the biggest reach (27.4M) for **SPM 43** — the strongest 'scale + intent' signal here.
- **The mainstream inclusion under-indexes, as expected:** *Bad Kingdom — Moderat (DJ Koze Remix)*, the most famous name in the set, scores just **SPM 8** — widely streamed, rarely saved as a discovery.
- **Two false-positive radios rejected by artist-description confirmation:** a *'Source of Uncertainty' radio* was seeded off an IDM track (desc: The Future Sound Of London, Amorphous Androgynous, Autechre), not KiNK; consistent with the Eminem *'Shake That'* (Solomun) and Passenger 10 *'Euphrasia'* (Adriatique) catches. Title-only radio hits must always be artist-confirmed.
- **Cross-artist arc across the series:** discovery footprint thins the deeper the selector — euro summer (current pop/dance) SPM 100–400 → old Solomun 3–100 (9/15 core have radios) → old Adriatique 10–30 (3/13) → **old Tale Of Us: 3/8 core have radios, and much of the catalogue isn't even on streaming.**
