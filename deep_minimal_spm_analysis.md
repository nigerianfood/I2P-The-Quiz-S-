## Methodology

**SPM (Saves Per Million)** = Radio Playlist Saves ÷ Seed Track Streams × 1,000,000

Streams-first: every play count resolved via the mystreamcount.com API, then title-only radio discovery, seed verification (`image_uri` path `/radio/track/{trackID}/en`), and save-count fetches. Full procedure in `SPM_METHODOLOGY.md`.

**Coverage.** 21 of 30 tracks have a dedicated auto-radio (verified seed) and are scored. **9 tracks** — mostly common one-word titles owned by other artists in search (Rush, Feel, Wish, Slide, Feel It, Big Beat, To Be Free) plus the accented *Prepárame* — surfaced no dedicated auto-radio in title search and are listed with resolved streams, pending the deeper sweep.

**Two duplicate-release seeds** accepted per methodology 4b, both confirmed via radio description: **Trapped - Radio-Edit** (Kolter; desc "With Kolter…") and **Best Be Believing** (AlunaGeorge; desc "With AlunaGeorge, Riordan…").

**Cross-playlist note:** Shinjuku, On 2nite, Trapped, and Rhythm Of The House also appear in the Beatport Top 100 analysis in this repo. Shinjuku here reads SPM 117 (2,383 saves / 20.4M streams) — its streams have grown to 20.4M (from 18.3M) while its SPM sits in the same diluting band flagged there (773→118).

## Key Findings

**Shinjuku (Franky Rizardo) owns the absolute-saves crown by a mile: 2,383 saves on 20.4M streams (SPM 117).** It's the playlist's stream anchor and its biggest discovery engine in raw terms — consistent with the Beatport read where it was diluting fast from a 773 peak.

**On 2nite (Silva Bumpa) is the second mass-scale signal: 1,387 saves on 14.1M (SPM 98).** A genuine club anthem pulling discovery-intent at scale.

**The curators seeded their own strongest early signals.** Locky & Luke Dean's opener **Check One, Check Two** lands at SPM 131 (12 saves / 91K) and the **Puff Puff Pass** Luke Dean remix at SPM 77 — small bases, but real intent on the hosts' own records.

**Move Shake (Jeff Sorkowitz) tops the ratio table at SPM 492**, but on 38 saves over 77K streams — the classic micro-base effect; directional, not scale-comparable to Shinjuku.

**Trapped (Kolter) at SPM 79** matches its Beatport-analysis read (66 there) — a steady minimal-house discovery signal on a duplicate-release seed, description-confirmed to Kolter.

**Three scored tracks have zero-save radios** (Show It Off, Something 2 Dance 2, Misbehaving) — auto-radios exist and seed correctly, but no listener has engaged yet.
